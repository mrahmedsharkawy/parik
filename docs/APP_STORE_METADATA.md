## App Review notes

`Bariq Gifts is a shopping app for personalized gifts and occasion products. Customers can browse without signing in. An account is required for saved profile information, order history, order tracking, notifications, and occasion reminders.`

`The app may ask for Photos/Camera access only when the customer chooses to attach a customization image. Location access is optional and is used only to help determine the delivery area when the customer has not entered an address. Notifications are optional and are used for order updates, offers, and occasion reminders.`

`Account deletion is available inside the app: Account > My Address > Delete My Account Permanently. The customer must type DELETE to confirm. The login account and personal profile data are deleted. Legally required order and invoice records are retained only after personal identifiers are removed.`

`Customized products may require 2–10 days for production depending on design and quantity. Purchases are for physical goods; the app does not sell digital content or subscriptions.`
